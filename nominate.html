What a seat is.

Twenty sire seats. Each seat is a permanent slot in the Vault — a $15,000 annual nomination that puts every foal by that sire into the Morgan Millions ledger for as long as the seat is held.

What the seat gets you

  • Every foal by your Vaulted sire is eligible for a share of the $1,000,000 target purse.
  • A 5% nominator fee on every placing your foals earn.
  • Right of first refusal on the seat in perpetuity, so long as annual dues are current.
  • Priority booking and preferred stallion positioning at the inaugural event.

How placement works

Class purses roll up under an 85 / 10 / 5 split: 85% to the foal owner, 10% to the trainer, 5% to the nominator. Four earners. One nomination.

The event

Target market Oklahoma City, October 6–8, 2027 (venue pending). Seven classes across the weekend, plus a $105,000 Crown Purse bonus layered on top.
