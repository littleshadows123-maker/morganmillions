The Crown Purse

$105,000 on top of the base.

A producing-sire bonus, sold at the December auction, paid at Friday finals. Fourteen ways to win. Up to $105,000 to a single Crown Holder if one sire sweeps the show.

The mechanic

How a Stallion Ticket works.

Every Crown-eligible sire issues exactly one Stallion Ticket per program year. That ticket is included with the auctioned stud fee for that sire at the December Stallion Service Auction.

Whoever wins the breeding becomes the Crown Holder for that sire's program year.

The ticket is on the sire, not on a specific foal. If any foal by that sire wins a class championship, the Crown Holder collects. If any foal takes reserve, the Crown Holder collects. Independent of who owns the winning foal.

The math

Per class. Per sire. Per show.

14 ways to win — Champion + Reserve × 7 classes.

A single sire cannot win the same class twice (Champion and Reserve go to two different horses), but the same sire can produce winners across multiple classes — and every one of those placings pays the Crown Holder.

Annual maximum: if a single sire sweeps every Champion and Reserve placing across all seven classes, the Crown Holder collects the full $105,000.

When and how it pays

Paid Friday. Same night.

Timing

Crown Purse is awarded during Friday finals — the same night the class purse is distributed. Reserve is always paid — no threshold, no cutoff.

Recipient

Payment goes to the Crown Holder (the December auction winner), regardless of who owns the foal. Double-dip allowed: if the Crown Holder also owns the winning foal, they collect the class purse and the Crown bonus on top.

Secondary market

Freely transferable.

Stallion Tickets are freely transferable all year, until championships are awarded Friday night. No lock-on event. The ticket follows the sire, not a foal.

  • Buy at the December auction, hold or resell as the foal crop develops.
  • Owners of promising foals can consolidate — buy the ticket for the sire of their contender.
  • Anyone can trade based on how a sire's foal crop is performing through the year.

The moment championships are decided Friday night, the ticket's value crystallizes — pay out or expire.

Who issues a ticket

Vaulted and Minted. Not Reserve.

Vaulted Sires

Every seat issues one ticket per year, at the December auction.

Minted Sires

Every sire in the Stallion Service Auction issues one ticket per year, same auction.

Reserve Sires

Do NOT issue tickets. Reserve is a foal-nomination path, not a stallion-tier path.

Bridge Open Roster

During Bridge years (2027–2030) only. See The Bridge →

What the Crown Purse is NOT

Not the base purse. Not a class.

  • Not paid out of the $1M base purse. The Crown Purse is a separate $105,000 pool, funded by the Auction Crown ticket sales.
  • Not a separate class. No one enters "the Crown." Any foal that wins a base-purse placing triggers a Crown payout for whoever holds that sire's ticket.
  • Not paid to the foal owner. The class purse (base) pays the foal owner. The Crown Purse pays the ticket holder. Two separate cheques from the same Friday-night ceremony.
