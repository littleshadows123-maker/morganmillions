MLF Dynamic GCH

World Champion. Homozygous black. Written into every foal he sires.

Vaulted Seat 02

Merriehill After Hours GCH

“Puck”

The most decorated Morgan of his generation.

Four World Champion Stallion titles. Three World Champion English Pleasure titles. A fourteen-year show career without a wasted season. Now inducted into the Show Horse Hall of Fame, Puck's foals are already writing the next chapter — nearly $1 million in a single sale, a record no Morgan has touched.

Jus' Sayin' CH

Unanimous World Champion at two. Then he went straight to the shed.

He walked into Oklahoma as a two-year-old and left as the unanimous World Champion Stallion. Named 2021 People's Choice Horse of the Year in his debut season. Thirty-four mares booked before his third birthday. A Salem Farm homebred whose future is measured in foal crops.
